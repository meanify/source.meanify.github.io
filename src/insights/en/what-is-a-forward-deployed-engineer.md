**A Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) is a software engineer who works directly inside a client's team, close to the users and the real problem, instead of building in isolation and handing over a finished product.** They write production code, but they also spend time understanding how the business works, so what they build fits the way people actually work.

## Where the term comes from

The role was made popular by data and software companies that sent engineers to work on site with their customers. Since then it has become common in AI companies, where every customer's data, processes and constraints are different, and the hard part is making technology work in a specific context.

The idea is simple: put the engineer where the problem is. Short feedback loops between users and the person writing the code lead to better software, delivered sooner.

## How it differs from other models

| | Consultant | Contractor or agency | In-house developer | Forward Deployed Engineer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Writes production code | Rarely | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Works inside your team | Sometimes | Rarely | Yes | Yes |
| Close to your users | Sometimes | Rarely | Yes | Yes |
| Brings outside experience | Yes | Sometimes | Rarely | Yes |
| Hiring time and cost | Low | Low | High | Low |

A Forward Deployed Engineer combines the closeness of an in-house developer with the experience and flexibility of an outside partner.

## What a Forward Deployed Engineer does day to day

- Joins your planning, stand-ups and reviews, in your tools.
- Works in your repositories, following your standards.
- Talks to the people who will use the software, to understand the real workflow.
- Builds in short iterations and demos progress often.
- Documents decisions and hands everything over, so your team can keep it running.

## Why the model suits AI projects

AI projects fail less often because of the model and more often because of the context: messy data, unclear processes, missing approvals, or a tool that nobody adopts. An engineer who sits close to the problem notices these issues early.

AI also changes how software is written. Tools can draft code, tests and documentation in minutes. That speed is only useful if someone with experience checks the result and understands what the business needs. A Forward Deployed Engineer does exactly that: uses AI to move fast, and takes responsibility for every change that ships. ## How Meanify works with Forward Deployed Engineers

At Meanify, every project is delivered by Forward Deployed Engineers who use validated AI workflows. In practice:

1. **Discovery:** a short workshop to agree on scope, priorities and what success looks like.
2. **Set-up:** engineers join your team and set up the AI-assisted workflow in your repositories.
3. **Build:** AI drafts code and tests; engineers design, review and refine every change.

## When to consider a Forward Deployed Engineer

The model is a good fit when:

- you need software built but can't wait months to hire a team;
- the problem is specific to your business and needs someone close to it;
- you want to use AI to move faster, but need someone accountable for quality;
- you want your own team to own and maintain the result.
